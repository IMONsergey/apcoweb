# Next UX candidates — not implemented in R3

## 1. Share or restore the selected plan

A landing-only URL such as `?billing=annually#plan-business` could restore the selected period and scroll to the relevant card after refresh or when shared with a colleague. This is the most useful next increment: it extends the contextual billing panel without adding another permanent control. Do not invent checkout parameters for the existing product.

## 2. Keep comparison labels visible

In the existing comparison dialog, retain the first column during horizontal scrolling and show edge hints only when content is hidden. Do not add another global floating panel.

## 3. Validated query examples

After search focus, offer a small optional row that inserts a real supported example without submitting it. Syntax and credit implications require product confirmation; do not invent filters.

Avoid timed sales popups, fake scarcity, forced FAQ opening, scrolling interception or an additional sticky CTA competing with billing.
