# Editorial image prompts

Built-in imagegen; three separate generated assets. No existing hero was overwritten. PNG masters were converted to responsive WebP files for the site without compositional edits.

## evidence.webp

Use case: stylized-concept. Create an editorial illustration for Apcosys, a professional internet infrastructure research site. Wide landscape 3:2 image. Subject: a precise physical archive of service observations: three offset translucent frosted glass plates on a cool-white studio surface, a single petrol-teal glass thread traveling between plates and one small solid teal marker linking the evidence. A subtle lens-like glass element reveals a few ordered silver-white records below. Elegant architectural product photography, close perspective, clear readable large forms, extremely restrained detail, spacious composition with subject filling 65% of frame. Pure cool whites #f6f6f6, silver gray, petrol teal #037a8f only. Strong dimensional lighting, realistic refraction, soft shadows, premium art direction. No warm tones, no beige, no gold, no purple, no people, no words, no logos, no UI, no charts, no dashboard, no cyberpunk. This is a conceptual illustration, not a precise diagram. Output a finished standalone site image.
